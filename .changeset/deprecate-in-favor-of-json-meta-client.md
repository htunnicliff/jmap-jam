**jmap-jam and jmap-rfc-types are deprecated.** They have been succeeded by [json-meta-client](https://json-meta-client.dev), and will not receive new features or fixes. The JMAP types from jmap-rfc-types now ship as `json-meta-client/rfc-types`. See the [migration guide](https://json-meta-client.dev/migrating-from-jmap-jam) to switch over.
